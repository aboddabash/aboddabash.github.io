Privacy Policy

RAQM by DABASH (“RAQM”, “the app”). Last updated: November 2026.

Your financial data

RAQM stores the transactions, budgets, goals and settings you enter on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. RAQM has no accounts and does not send your financial data to our servers. You can export your data as a CSV or delete it at any time from Settings. Clearing your browser data also removes it.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve, personalize and measure ads, where permitted and in line with your consent choices. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
